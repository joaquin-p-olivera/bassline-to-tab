Bassline to Tab

Choose a song and get its bass line as tablature. Everything runs on this device: the bass is separated with Demucs (on the GPU) and transcribed with Basic Pitch. Your audio is never uploaded.

Advanced
Lower finds quieter notes (and more false ones).

The first separation downloads the Demucs model (~84 MB). It is cached for next time.

    Tap a bar in the tab to jump there.
