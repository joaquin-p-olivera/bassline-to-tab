Bass & Guitar to Tab

Fretboard notes Tune your bass Bass amp

Choose a song and get its bass line, its guitar line or both as tablature. Everything runs on this device: the instruments are separated with Demucs (on the GPU) and transcribed with Basic Pitch. Your audio is never uploaded. Guitar is a first test: one note at a time, so riffs and solos work better than chords.
